Privacy Policy - Murmur

Effective date: 2026-11-24
Contact: (add your support email before submission)

Murmur is a local-first, privacy-first voice notes app. This policy exists
to satisfy App Store Connect / Google Play requirements - the short version
is: **your data never leaves your device unless you explicitly send it to an
AI provider you chose and configured yourself.**

What Murmur stores

Everything Murmur creates stays on your device:

  • Audio recordings (.m4a files) - stored in the app's private documents directory.
  • Transcripts, AI-formatted outputs, titles, tags, folders, reminders - stored in a local SQLite database on-device.
  • API keys - stored only in the platform keychain (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore via flutter_secure_storage).

Murmur operates **no backend servers, no user accounts, and no analytics
collection**. We receive nothing from your use of the app.

What leaves your device (only when you ask)

When you tap an AI action (Clean Note, Checklist, Email, Summary, or a custom
prompt), the text transcript of that note is sent directly from your
device to the AI provider you selected - Google Gemini, Groq, OpenAI, or your
own custom/local endpoint - using your own API key.

  • Murmur shows a one-time consent dialog before the first send to each

    • provider, disclosing exactly which provider receives the text.

  • Your audio file is never uploaded by V1 features.
  • The provider's own privacy policy governs that request:
  • Google Gemini API: https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms
  • Groq: https://groq.com/privacy-policy/
  • OpenAI: https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/

If you never add an API key, nothing is ever transmitted - all recording,
storage, and the offline "Clean Note" action work entirely on-device.

OS speech recognition

Live transcription uses the operating system's built-in speech service
(Apple Speech / Android SpeechRecognizer), which may process audio on-device
or via the OS vendor's servers under your OS settings - that traffic is
between your device and Apple/Google, never through Murmur.

Deleting your data

Settings → "Wipe all local data & keys" permanently deletes every note,
recording, transcript, AI output, API key and preference in one tap.
Uninstalling the app also removes all app data from the device.

Purchases

The optional one-time "Lifetime Pro" unlock is processed by the app store
(Apple App Store / Google Play) via RevenueCat. Murmur receives only an
anonymous entitlement status - never your name, card, or purchase details.

Children

Murmur does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including
children under 13.

Changes

This policy may be updated for new features; material changes are noted here.
