Privacy Policy - Brusio

Effective date: 2026-11-24
Provider: Fendo Technologies Limited
Contact: okolomiracle513@gmail.com

Brusio is a local-first, privacy-first voice notes app by Fendo Technologies Limited. The short version is: your recordings, transcripts and notes never leave your device unless you explicitly send them to an AI provider you chose and configured yourself. The only data we receive is the anonymous analytics and crash reporting described below.

What Brusio stores

Everything Brusio creates stays on your device:

  • Audio recordings (.m4a files) - stored in the app's private documents directory.
  • Transcripts, AI-formatted outputs, titles, tags, folders, reminders - stored in a local SQLite database on-device.
  • API keys - stored only in the platform keychain (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore via flutter_secure_storage).

Brusio operates no backend servers and no user accounts. Your content stays on your device.

Analytics & crash reporting

Brusio uses Google Firebase to collect a small amount of anonymous, non-personal telemetry so we can keep the app stable:

  • Firebase Analytics - anonymous app-usage events (for example: a recording started, a transcription ran, the paywall was shown) and an anonymous install identifier. Events never contain your audio, transcripts, note text, titles, or API keys.
  • Firebase Crashlytics - crash logs, stack traces and basic device diagnostics (model, OS version, free storage) sent when the app fails.

This data is processed by Google under the Firebase privacy policy. It is not linked to your identity, is never sold or shared for advertising, and contains no note content.

What leaves your device (only when you ask)

When you tap an AI action (Clean Note, Checklist, Email, Summary, or a custom
prompt), the text transcript of that note is sent directly from your
device to the AI provider you selected - Google Gemini, Groq, OpenAI, or your
own custom/local endpoint - using your own API key.

  • Brusio shows a one-time consent dialog before the first send to each provider, disclosing exactly which provider receives the text.
  • If you choose a cloud transcription engine (Groq, OpenAI or Gemini), the audio file itself is uploaded to that provider for transcription - again after an explicit consent prompt, and again using your own key. On-device Whisper and OS transcription send nothing.
  • The provider's own privacy policy governs each request:
  • Google Gemini API: https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms
  • Groq: https://groq.com/privacy-policy/
  • OpenAI: https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/

If you never add an API key, none of your content is ever transmitted - all recording, storage, transcription and the offline "Clean Note" action work entirely on-device. Only the anonymous analytics and crash telemetry above still applies.

OS speech recognition

Live transcription uses the operating system's built-in speech service
(Apple Speech / Android SpeechRecognizer), which may process audio on-device
or via the OS vendor's servers under your OS settings - that traffic is
between your device and Apple/Google, never through Brusio.

Deleting your data

Settings → "Wipe all local data & keys" permanently deletes every note,
recording, transcript, AI output, API key and preference in one tap.
Uninstalling the app also removes all app data from the device.

Purchases

The optional one-time "Lifetime Pro" unlock is processed by the app store
(Apple App Store / Google Play) via RevenueCat. Brusio receives only an
anonymous entitlement status - never your name, card, or purchase details.

Children

Brusio does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including
children under 13.

Changes

This policy may be updated for new features; material changes are noted here.
