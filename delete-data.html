Data Deletion - Brusio

Brusio stores all user data locally on your device only. There are no Brusio accounts and no Brusio servers holding your data, so there is nothing server-side to delete and no retention period applies.

How to delete your data

  1. Open Brusio → Settings → "Wipe all local data & keys" permanently deletes every note, audio recording, transcript, AI output, API key, and preference - in one tap.
  2. Alternatively, uninstalling the app removes all app data from the device.

What is deleted

  • Voice recordings (.m4a files)
  • Notes, transcripts, AI outputs, checklists
  • Folders, tags, preferences
  • API keys stored in the device keychain/keystore

What is retained

  • By Brusio: nothing - no data exists outside your device.
  • By Apple/Google: purchase records are held by the app stores under their own policies (Brusio receives only an anonymous entitlement status).
  • By AI providers you configured: audio/text sent to Groq, OpenAI, or Gemini is governed by that provider's retention policy; request deletion directly with them if needed.
